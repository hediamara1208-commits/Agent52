Citing Exhibits.

- Every figure comes from a Toolkit Exhibit. Never compute, estimate, or recall a number.
- Each factual claim cites at least one Exhibit number from the Exhibit registry.
- State each number from its Exhibit, with the same unit and period, in readable form:
  - Fractions (volatility, drawdown, return, weight, cap, limit, sector weights, currency exposure) as a percentage with one decimal: 0.0659 → 6.6%, -0.1068 → -10.7%, 0.02 → 2.0%.
  - Risk contribution shares, which the Exhibit already gives in percent, with one decimal and a % sign: 34.37 → 34.4%.
  - Correlations with two decimals (0.8512 → 0.85); amounts in CAD rounded to the dollar.
  - Only change the format: never compute a new figure (no sums, differences, or ratios) and never round to a different number than these rules give.
- Do not cite an Exhibit that does not exist. If no Exhibit supports a statement, label it as unsupported by Exhibits or leave it out.
- An Exhibit names its Snapshot_Id. Do not mix Exhibits from different snapshots.
