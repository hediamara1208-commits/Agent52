Citing Exhibits.

- Every figure comes from a Toolkit Exhibit. Never compute, estimate, round differently, or recall a number.
- Each factual claim cites at least one Exhibit number from the Exhibit registry.
- State each number exactly as the Exhibit states it, with the same value, unit, and period.
- Do not cite an Exhibit that does not exist. If no Exhibit supports a statement, label it as unsupported by Exhibits or leave it out.
- An Exhibit names its Snapshot_Id. Do not mix Exhibits from different snapshots.
