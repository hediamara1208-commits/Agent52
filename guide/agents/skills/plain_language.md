Plain language.

- Write in English, in plain words, for a non-expert reader.
- Every summary is at most 60 words.
- Define a technical term the first time you use it, or use an everyday word instead.
- One idea per sentence. Avoid jargon, hype, and filler.
- Never show raw decimals such as 0.0659 to the reader: write 6.6% (see Citing Exhibits).
